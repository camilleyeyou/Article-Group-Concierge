<objective>
Rewrite the Opus grader's inputs and outputs (EVAL-02, EVAL-03, rubric half of EVAL-04, grader half of EVAL-01).

Purpose: the grader currently sees the persona profile, the `expected` block and the query type, none of which the
app sees (eval/grade.ts buildPrompt). Its component rule says "4-10 components" while the prompt says "Typical deck:
4-8 components, never exceed 10"; its CTA rule rewards "follow-up questions and/or Strategy Lead" and its fallback
rule penalised missing Strategy Lead offers (baseline reasons for p3-q07, p4-q07, p5-q01..q04), although the app only
offers the Strategy Lead when retrieval is empty. Unsupported claims are a flat string list, so the report can't
split them by kind.

The rubric moves into eval/rubric.ts so the second grader (Plan 06) uses the identical rubric, prompt and schema.
The judge model stays Opus 5.5 (EVAL_MODEL default 'claude-opus-5-5'); do not change it.

Output: eval/rubric.ts, eval/rubric.test.ts, updated eval/grade.ts and eval/lib.ts.
</objective>
