<objective>
Find every piece of work on articlegroup.com/work that is not in the index and ingest the approved items (DATA-02, D-01..D-04).

Purpose: the concierge can only present work that is indexed. The eval already has 4 queries (p3-q01, p3-q06, p4-q03, p4-q10)
targeting site-only work (Salesforce partner ecosystem, Simons Foundation education division, "A world of perspectives on the
future of learning").

Verified facts (2026-11-21):
- `https://articlegroup.com/work` returns a Cloudflare 403 to curl; `https://www.articlegroup.com/work` returns 200 with a browser
  User-Agent and `Accept: text/html`. It lists 18 `/work/<slug>/` links. Do not try to bypass a 403 beyond these headers; stop and ask.
- Pages are server-rendered; text extracts cleanly after stripping script/style and nav/footer lines. Some pages embed Vimeo
  (`player.vimeo.com/video/<id>`), e.g. /work/simons-foundation/ embeds vimeo 190925687.
- `AG Website RAG case studies.pdf` pp. 27-29 are Salesforce/Slack partner enablement, Slalom and Salesforce/WhatsApp, all
  already indexed. So /work/salesforce/ ("Supporting the largest partner ecosystem", an 8-year partner content program) is a
  NEAR-MATCH to `salesforce-slack-strategic-sales-enablement`; the user decides whether it is separate work.
- The Salesforce page quotes third-party figures ("$1 trillion in new business revenues", "4.2 Million Jobs"). These are
  Salesforce ecosystem forecasts, not AG results; flag such text so later phases cannot present it as an AG outcome.
- /work/houselogic-project-title-goes-here-for-now/ has a placeholder-looking slug; flag it.

Output: diff script, approval table, ingest script, new case_study documents for approved rows.
Cost: embeddings only (a few dozen chunks, < $0.01).
</objective>
